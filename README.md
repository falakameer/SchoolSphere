# SchoolSphere

### An India-first student profile, opportunities & career-readiness platform for Classes 8–12.

SchoolSphere is a product venture exploring how students can build a structured, living record of their academic and extracurricular journey, discover relevant opportunities, and make more informed career decisions.

---

## The Problem

For students in Classes 8–12, important parts of their journey are fragmented:

- Achievements and certificates are scattered across PDFs, files and WhatsApp groups.
- Scholarships, competitions and other opportunities are often discovered informally or by chance.
- Career decisions are frequently driven by exams, coaching and conventional streams rather than a student's interests, strengths and aspirations.
- Parents often want to support their children but lack a unified view of their development and opportunities.

SchoolSphere is being built around this gap.

---

## The Concept

SchoolSphere brings together three core layers:

### 1. Student Profile
A structured, living profile covering:

- Academics
- Projects
- Extracurricular activities
- Sports & arts
- Leadership
- Interests
- Achievements
- Exams & aspirations

### 2. Opportunity Layer

A curated experience for discovering relevant:

- Scholarships
- Competitions
- Programs
- Events
- Academic opportunities
- Career-related opportunities

Opportunities can be filtered around factors such as class, interests and examination goals.

### 3. Career Readiness & Networking

A safe, moderated environment designed to help students:

- Explore potential career paths
- Learn from relevant mentors and alumni
- Discover peers and opportunities beyond their own school
- Build networking skills before entering college and the professional world

Parents and schools remain part of the ecosystem.
